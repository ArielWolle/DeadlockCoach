## 1. What lategame is
- [WIKI] Lategame = players looking to end the game; marked by taking map objectives, sieging the enemy base, and long respawn timers.
- [WIKI] Respawn ~38s at 20 min, 70s at 30, 85s at 40, cap 90s. A pick late is worth a large window of map pressure.

## 2. Structure order and facts
- [WIKI] Per team: 3 Guardians, 3 Walkers, 6 Base Guardians (pairs), 2 Shrines, 1 Patron. Win = destroy enemy Patron.
- [WIKI] Base Guardians prevent damage to Shrines until at least one set is destroyed; "Shrines are invulnerable to damage until a pair of Base Guardians has been destroyed" (Backdoor Protection page).
- [WIKI] Shrines protect the Patron until both are destroyed. Each Shrine: 2000 souls, heals its partner, sends stronger enemy troopers down its lane.
- [WIKI] Base Guardians and Shrines have extra Bullet Resist (40% / 60%) that decays by 20% for each enemy hero nearby, to 0%. (Having your whole team near drops it quickly.)
- [WIKI] Super Troopers (+60% dmg, +40% HP) after a Shrine in that lane is destroyed.
- [WIKI] Base Guardian trooper DPS vs them 80 (resist 67.5), Shrine 50 (resist 0).
- [WIKI] Troopers gain 35% damage resistance near/in the enemy base. At 35 min, troopers +50% HP.

## 3. Patron
- [WIKI] Phase 1: 12000 base HP, 120 HP/s regen out of combat (only structure that regens fully), unkillable until both Shrines are destroyed. Laser beam 440 + 5% HP spirit DPS that also lowers bullet/spirit resist 10%; shrine defense explosion after 3.5s delay (300 + 20% HP) that curses.
- [WIKI] Phase 1 max health grows by 250/min after 20 minutes.
- [WIKI] Killing phase 1 triggers the Last Stand Buff: defenders' remaining respawn times shortened (>=30s reduced by 20s; 10-30s -> 10s; <=10s unchanged), applied once.
- [WIKI] Patron phase 2 ("Weakened Patron"): a floating orb in the pit. 12000 base HP +450/min after 1 minute. Damage Pulse 75/s to up to 3 enemies within 19m. Pit Explosion every 15s (300 + 20% HP, curse, 3.5s delay).
- [WIKI] Weakened Patron: immune to damage from outside the pit; 50% damage resistance when one defender has been in the pit >2s; 100% resistance when two or more defenders are in the pit. (Key: kill or push defenders out of the pit before committing damage.)
- [WIKI] Several abilities do reduced damage to the Patron (50% for Bebop's Hyper Beam, Infernus' Flame Dash, McGinnis's Heavy Barrage, Mina's Nox Nostra, Paradox's Pulse Grenade, Pocket's Affliction, Seven's Storm Cloud, Sinclair's Vexing Bolt, Solomon's Setup Bishop, Victor's Aura of Suffering, Warden's Last Stand; 20% for Haze's Fixation).
- [WIKI] After Patron phase 1 dies, the enemy team's respawns drastically shorten (Strategy page); also gains damage resistance while enemy players are in radius.

## 4. Backdoor
- [WIKI] Backdoor Protection applies to Walkers, Base Guardians, Shrines and Patron (not Lane Guardians outside Street Brawl). Indicated by a green circle and "Protected" text with a green heart.
- [WIKI] Protection resist 65% and regen 65 HP/s (Patron regen 75). Protection is removed by pushing the trooper wave up to the structure, or for base structures by having any zipline pushed up to the base. Re-applies after a 14s buffer.
- [WIKI] Backdoor regen only heals back to the HP the structure had before the protected attack.
- [WIKI] Glossary: backdoor = attacking an objective without allied troopers present.
- [COMMUNITY] None of the fetched guides addressed backdooring tactics explicitly. UNVERIFIED for ranked best practice.
- [INFERENCE] A one-person backdoor is only viable when one hero's DPS exceeds the regen+resist floor; otherwise bring waves.

## 5. Rejuvenator usage
- [WIKI] Rejuvenator drops after Midboss dies. Credits last 3 minutes (reduced from 4). Up to 3 credits, one per heavy melee hit. Revives in place after 3s with 100% health.
- [WIKI] Killing a player with a credit does not show as a kill and gives no souls (but triggers on-kill effects). The reviving player is "dead" for ally-presence effects and does not contest the Rift.
- [WIKI] Warns when 30s remain on the buff.
- [WIKI] Either team may claim credits after Midboss death; stealing is possible.
- [COMMUNITY] Not covered by fetched guides. [INFERENCE] The best use is to push into base immediately after taking it so all 3 credits convert to survivals during the siege, i.e. start the Midboss only when you can push with 3 min of buff.
- [INFERENCE] Notice minimap/top-bar credit icons: "credits left" indicator is HUD-observable (top UI bar shows remaining credits; players who haven't consumed one are highlighted below net worth).

## 6. Throw prevention
- [COMMUNITY] "Don't throw a won game by chasing one last kill into the enemy base - take the objective, then reset. Greed at this stage loses more leads than anything else" (metabot).
- [COMMUNITY] When ahead, "skip the coin-flip fight", take free objectives and force the enemy to fight you into your cover (metabot).
- [COMMUNITY] A fight entered with team split or a teammate still walking from base is usually lost (metabot).
- [WIKI] Dying late is expensive: long respawns, and an enemy Patron kill ends the game.
- [WIKI] Last Stand Buff: after you kill Patron phase 1, defenders respawn quickly. So do not stagger deaths in phase 2.
- [WIKI] Glossary: Throw = intentionally sabotaging; "Feed" = overextending and dying to give the enemy an advantage.
- [INFERENCE] Throw-risk heuristics for the coach: late game clock, team has soul lead, player alone near enemy base with enemy members not visible on minimap, respawn timer > 40s.

## 7. Closing checklist ([INFERENCE] assembled from wiki mechanics)
1. Push waves so zipline reaches base (removes protection).
2. Group with Rejuvenator credits if available.
3. Bring numbers to the Base Guardians (resist decays with nearby enemy heroes).
4. Kill Shrines (partner heals if left standing, so kill both quickly).
5. Hit Patron phase 1; when it falls, expect defenders to respawn quickly (Last Stand).
6. Enter the pit with numbers; defenders in the pit give the Weakened Patron 50%/100% damage resistance, so clear the pit first.
7. Retreat/reset if two or more of your team are dead and the enemy is full.

## 8. Ranked context (wiki)
- [WIKI] Ranked: ~300 rank points per match; abandon rules: before 5 min match unscored; after 5 min only leaver's team safe to leave unless they win. 3 bans (Standard 2). Season Beta 1 ends 2026-11-05.
- Not strategy, but explains why throws matter: win streak bonus from the 3rd consecutive win.

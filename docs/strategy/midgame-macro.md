## 1. What midgame is
- [WIKI] Midgame = right after laning ends; players roam, look to solidify leads. Players from each lane may join at different times. Ends when a substantial advantage is gained (large soul lead, multiple walkers down) and the team looks to end.
- [WIKI] Macro = decisions about the map, pressure, objectives. Concepts: map knowledge, timings, uptime/presence, pressure.
- [WIKI] Uptime: time spent contributing (farming, harassing, pushing). Downtime: respawning, idly waiting, aimless rotation.

## 3. Timers
- [WIKI] Urn: first at 10:00 (descends 12.5s), then every 5 min, alternates Yellow(York)/Green(Greenwich), first always Yellow. Starts walking itself after 3 min unpicked.
- [WIKI] Unstable Rift: first spawn time varies 10-12 min (nominal 11:00), then 6-8 min after the previous (nominal 7). Sequence: world-only VFX on a random side-lane bridge -> 60s -> global announcement + minimap -> 20s -> opens with a 20m capture circle.
- [WIKI] Mid-Boss: now spawned at game start (update 2026-05-22). Respawns 7 min after first death, 6 after second, 5 afterward. Announced by roar plus "Midboss has arrived."
- [WIKI] Bridge buffs every 5:00. Powerups 2 locations from 5:00, every 5 min; replace if uncollected.
- [WIKI] Sinner's Sacrifice camps from 8:00, respawn 5 min after clearing.
- [WIKI] Guardians lose all damage resistance at 12:00; Walkers at 18:00.
- [WIKI] Waves 25s at 20:00 and 20s at 35:00; troopers +50% HP at 35:00.
- [WIKI] Tier 2 permanent buffs from 10:00; Tier 3 from 30:00.
- [WIKI] Respawn timer: ~8s at 5 min, 38s at 20, 70s at 30, 85s at 40, cap 90s. Dying to a non-player unit +10s. Comeback kill rule: after 10 min killing a player >15% above the killer team's average net worth adds +6s to +22s respawn.

## 4. Taking Walkers (the midgame goal)
- [WIKI] Extra slots unlock per Walker destroyed: 1 -> bottom left, 2 -> top right, 3 -> bottom right. Base slot count is 9, flex 3.
- [WIKI] Walker has backdoor protection: 65% damage resist and 65 HP/s regen when no enemy troopers are near. Protection is removed by pushing the trooper wave up to the structure and has a 14s buffer before re-applying. Destroying a structure instantly pushes your zipline to it.
- [WIKI] Walker backdoor example: a hero needs more than ~189 DPS to dent a backdoored walker with no other resists (wiki formula: base DPS x (1 - 0.65) - 65).
- [WIKI] After a Shrine falls, troopers on that lane (and Blue lane once either shrine falls) become Super Troopers (+60% damage, +40% HP; 15% less bounty).
- [WIKI] Walker trooper damage multiplier is low (troopers deal 24 DPS, walker resists 80% of trooper damage in the combat table), so heroes must do the work while troopers tank.
- [COMMUNITY] "Taking down a Walker after a Guardian falls awards an Extra Slot, one of the highest-value post-lane objectives. When a Guardian goes down, push troopers to the Walker rather than spreading thin" (games.gg).

## 5. Rotations and presence
- [WIKI] Plan leaving lane after securing the wave; return before next wave.
- [WIKI] Ziplines extend only as far as allied troopers pushed; rotate along pushed lanes.
- [WIKI] Teleporters (4, linked in pairs) traverse horizontally but have a wind-up where pursuers can catch you.
- [WIKI] Missing: pinging an enemy portrait (Tab + middle-click) warns allies the enemy is unaccounted for.
- [COMMUNITY] "Rotate on crash windows, not randomly"; "convert won fights into the nearest permanent objective"; shop after major value spikes (games.gg).
- [COMMUNITY] Before any group objective: check which teammates are near, ults ready, waves cleared; assign simple jobs; hold an escape path; after the objective, spend souls and reset (deadlockatlas).

## 6. Splitting / ratting
- [WIKI] Glossary: ratting = separating from the team to push lanes/take an objective while the enemy team is busy elsewhere. Punish = killing an enemy at a disadvantaged position (overextended, ratting, stealing camps).
- [WIKI] Super troopers and rift troopers apply pressure and force responses (Rift Troopers drop no souls).
- [COMMUNITY] Lone wandering is a beginner mistake (deadlockhandbook listing, not read). UNVERIFIED guidance on when splitting is correct; no source supplied precise rules.
- [INFERENCE] Safe to split when minimap shows all five enemies on the other side or dead/respawning; call "missing" if one is not visible.

## 7. Soul Urn decisions
- [WIKI] Pick up by hitting it with a melee attack (light or heavy).
- [WIKI] Courier: +2 m/s move speed (cap 15), +1 stamina, +10% dash distance, +25% stamina regen, 100% slow resist. Trailing team gets resists and +5 m/s sprint scaling with deficit (max at 15% net worth difference).
- [WIKI] Courier cannot use ziplines, teleporters, parry. Melee hits and stuns force a drop.
- [WIKI] Souls are released as orbs and can be denied; if not denied all go to the courier; an ally securing an orb shares with the courier.
- [WIKI] If not delivered within 45s of pick-up it decays over another 45s.
- [WIKI] Urn value is set at spawn; holding does not increase it.
- [WIKI] Comeback: Urn gives more souls to the trailing team.
- [INFERENCE] Decision: take urn when the enemy cannot reach the delivery side; escort if the enemy has "missing" players; deny orbs at the drop point.

## 8. Unstable Rift decisions
- [WIKI] Capture takes 12s with at least one player in the circle; both teams earn progress; claim only when alone. Contested for >50s warns; 60s ends in a draw (13 orbs released, unclaimed souls lost).
- [WIKI] Trooper counters: troopers cannot contest. Players reviving with Rejuvenator or sent away by Doorman do not contest.
- [WIKI] Trailing team: capture time 6s (50% faster) and leading team 18s; resist aura within 35m.
- [WIKI] Winning team gets Rift Troopers (min 5, up to 14 for trailing winner). Rift Troopers have +100% health/damage on first rift (+20% each, cap 160%), 25% melee resist, 30%+ spirit resist, no souls.
- [WIKI] Players in the zone are revealed on the minimap.
- [INFERENCE] Treat it as a team fight spot: arrive grouped by the 20s window after the announcement.

## 9. Mid-Boss decisions
- [WIKI] Stats: 13000 base HP, +195 HP per minute since spawn, 15 HP/s regen, 15% bullet resist, 35% debuff resist, shield starts at 35 HP +5/min. It only damages those inside its pit and only takes damage from players inside it. Laser beam 100 DPS, 38m range, targets the closest player.
- [WIKI] Roars at 50% health, alerting both teams.
- [WIKI] On kill: 3000 +50/min souls split equally, 2 permanent buffs per player, Rejuvenator drops (6s descent).
- [WIKI] Either team can steal the Rejuvenator after the kill by heavy-meleeing the crystal.
- [WIKI] Rejuvenator Credits: up to 3 per kill, one per heavy melee hit; buff lasts 3 minutes; each player revives in place after 3s with 100% health, consuming one credit (one per player).
- [WIKI] Dying while Midboss is or is about to be available matters (Strategy page: "Care should be taken not to die while Midboss is (or is soon-to-be) available").
- [WIKI] Dying to Midboss increases respawn +10s (non-player kill).
- [INFERENCE] Boss decision checklist: all enemies visible or dead? Team grouped? Ults available? Enemy near the pit for steal? Do not start if you cannot hold the crystal.

## 10. Economy tie-ins for midgame
- [WIKI] Soul lead: leading in laning gives you the ultimate earlier. Funneling and box-running/jungling are ways to boost a carry.
- [WIKI] After 8 minutes the two lowest net-worth players on each team get passive catch-up souls (2.5% and 1.5% of team income).
- See economy-and-buy-order.md.

## 11. Common midgame errors (community, [COMMUNITY])
- Kills with no objective follow-up (metabot).
- Chasing one more kill instead of taking the objective, then losing the lead (metabot).
- Ignoring waves before an objective (deadlockatlas).
- Arriving late after losing a full wave (deadlockatlas).

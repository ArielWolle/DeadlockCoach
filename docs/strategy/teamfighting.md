## 1. Wiki-grounded mechanics that shape fights
- [WIKI] Cover: most skills/items need line of sight; use corners/walls. Right-shoulder camera means right-side peeks expose less.
- [WIKI] Positioning in fights: "staying safe but active ... will allow you to apply pressure to the enemy while removing the opportunity for them to gain an advantage by killing you."
- [WIKI] Hero kill souls are shared among nearby assisters (damage within 10s): 1 player 125%, 2 players 57% each, 3 -> 28% each, etc. Killing blow gets a bonus for 2+ players. So fighting as a group raises total souls (114% for 2, 84% for 3) while dividing per head.
- [WIKI] Killing a hero richer than your team's average gives up to +126.8% more souls (comeback). Kill stronger heroes for outsized rewards.
- [WIKI] Respawn grows to 38s by 20 min; kills create map pressure.
- [WIKI] Rejuvenator credits: a killed player revives in place after 3s with full health; killing them gives no souls. Count credits before committing.
- [WIKI] Assist counts if damaged within 10s.
- [WIKI] Glossary: Pick = killing a separated enemy / targeting low health or high threat to gain a numbers advantage. Burst, CC, squishy, frontliner, support, carry, assassin.
- [WIKI] Unstable Rift / Midboss pits restrict who can be damaged (Midboss pit: only players inside it can damage it).

## 2. Parry and melee (fight-relevant)
- [WIKI] Parry key F (default). Parry state lasts 1s; incoming melee is parried for the first 0.75s. A successful parry stuns the attacker for 2.75s (not reducible by debuff resist), blocks melee damage, and makes them take +25% damage from all sources. Missed parry cooldown 4.5s. Unstoppable prevents the stun.
- [WIKI] After a successful parry there is a 0.3s window of no actions.
- [WIKI] Heavy melee has a wind-up and forward lunge, so it can be reacted to by parrying; riskier than a light melee. Heavy melee cooldown 1s (1.3s on miss).
- [WIKI] Melee on a climbing player causes an 80% slow ramping down to 20% over 2s; on an urn carrier forces a drop.
- [WIKI] Parry can block Troopers (stuns them) and Guardians; it cannot normally parry Walkers or most abilities.
- [WIKI] Heavy Melee Cancel (using Fleetfoot etc.) can bait enemy parries.
- [COMMUNITY] Beginner mistake list on deadlockhandbook includes "bad parries" (listing only; content not read). UNVERIFIED details.

## 3. Positioning
- [COMMUNITY] Front-to-back: durable disruptors in front, damage dealers behind with escape lanes; do not stand in front of your tank (metabot).
- [COMMUNITY] Spread so one AoE ultimate cannot hit everyone; but close enough to help (metabot, deadlockatlas).
- [COMMUNITY] Use elevation and cover; fighting uphill into a dug-in enemy feeds picks (metabot).
- [COMMUNITY] Pick one "job" before the fight: start contact, protect a carry, control an entrance, follow initiation, or finish a target (deadlockatlas).
- [COMMUNITY] Weapon heroes keep a firing lane; spirit heroes cast where movement is predictable and keep a second rotation (deadlockatlas).
- [WIKI] Movement tools: air jump (1 stamina), air dash, wall jump, mantle, down dash, slide. No fall damage. Sprint begins after 5s out of combat and persists until entering combat or zooming in. Stamina Mastery item (Vitality) expands air jump/dash/down dash counts to two.
- [WIKI] Steam vents make you invisible and untargetable (to explicit targets) with small regen; Juke Rooms are dead ends used for hiding/ambush; Healing Snacks give a small heal.
- [WIKI] Plaza fog drains stamina and blocks vision/hearing in the Sunken Plaza (Cursed Apple).

## 4. Engage / disengage
- [COMMUNITY] Engage only with an advantage: pick available, key ability landed, ult advantage, numbers/respawns, enemy mid-objective (metabot). No advantage -> reset.
- [COMMUNITY] Group before committing; pick the fight location; open with disruption not damage; commit or disengage, don't dither (metabot).
- [COMMUNITY] If the opener whiffs, retreat together; staggered deaths give repeated advantages (deadlockatlas).
- [COMMUNITY] After a won fight, count survivors and take the highest-value safe action (objective, wave, reset) rather than chasing (deadlockatlas).
- [WIKI] Punish: killing an enemy at a disadvantageous position (overextending, stealing camps, ratting).
- [INFERENCE] HUD signals for "advantage to engage": enemy portraits greyed/respawn timers on the top HUD; enemy ult icons (if visible); allied count near objective on minimap.

## 5. Target priority
- [COMMUNITY] Priority (metabot): exposed backliner > key ability-carry > low-health anyone > disruptors > tank frontliner (only when nothing else reachable). Reachability overrides raw threat.
- [COMMUNITY] Focus the same target as the team; call one target per fight. Don't pour bullets into a defensive (e.g. Metal Skin) or CC into an Unstoppable.
- [WIKI] Counter items: Metal Skin, Return Fire, Battle Vest, Weapon Shielding, Bullet Resilience vs high burst weapon damage; Healbane/Decay/Toxic Bullets vs healing; Debuff Reducer/Dispel Magic/Divine Barrier vs DoT or CC; Slowing Hex vs mobility; Silence Wave/Silencer vs ability reliance.
- [WIKI] Comeback: killing a rich enemy gives up to 126.8% more souls.

## 6. Ultimate usage
- [WIKI] Ultimates unlockable at 3.8k souls (enemy ultimates "significantly tilt fights in lane" when they hit it).
- [COMMUNITY] Don't waste cooldowns on the first visible target; hold control for the diver, hold defense for when focused (deadlockatlas). Ult advantage = engage reason (metabot).
- [WIKI] Some ultimates scale/reset on hero kills (Shiv's Killing Blow, Grey Talon, Mo & Krill, Vindicta ultimates) - these also trigger on Rejuvenator-credit "kills".
- [INFERENCE] Hero-specific ult advice is out of scope; no wiki hero pages fetched. UNVERIFIED.

## 7. Spirit vs gun fights
- [WIKI] Melee damage benefits from 50% of weapon damage buffs; "M1" refers to weapon-based heroes/builds (Glossary).
- [WIKI] Counter-buy examples: gun burst heroes (Haze, Venator) -> bullet resist items; DoT heroes -> debuff removers; Silencer reduces spirit damage.
- [WIKI] Troopers/Guardians: Spirit resist on troopers rises over time (20% at 25 min, 70% at 50 min).
- [COMMUNITY] Spirit vs weapon is covered by metabot "Weapon vs Spirit Builds" (not fetched). UNVERIFIED.
- [COMMUNITY] Weapon heroes avoid walking into control for one extra shot; spirit heroes preserve a second rotation (deadlockatlas).

## 8. HUD-observable fight signals ([INFERENCE])
- Allied health bars (team panel), dead players/respawn icons.
- Minimap: allied cluster vs enemy cluster; "missing" enemies.
- Ability cooldown icons on own bar; stamina bars under crosshair.
- Top-bar Rejuvenator credit indicators.
- Damage numbers and kill feed.
- Objective timers (Urn, Rift, Midboss).

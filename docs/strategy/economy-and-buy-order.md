## 1. Souls = currency + XP
- [WIKI] Souls are both currency and XP (boons). Every player starts with 600.
- [WIKI] Level (boon) thresholds are identical for all heroes; each level-up grants a boon; levels 0, 2, 4, 7 grant an ability unlock, other levels grant an ability point (max 32). Max boon level 35. Past max, HUD "levels" every 2000 souls without boons.
- [WIKI] Ultimate can be unlocked at 3.8k souls.
- [WIKI] Boons increase base bullet damage, base melee damage, spirit power, base health. Some abilities/items have boon scaling.
- [WIKI] Souls shown on the character UI = secured + haunted.

## 3. Haunted vs secured souls
- [WIKI] Souls from neutral sources (haunts, sinner's sacrifices, crates, urn delivery) are "haunted": lost on death. If you die with at least 50 + 5/min haunted souls a Soul Bag drops (claimable by a heavy melee, despawns after 3 min); less than that is permanently lost.
- [WIKI] Haunted souls convert to secured at 0.5%/s of the current haunted total plus a flat base (1.6/s at start, scaling with the 8%/min global bounty growth). Example: 1000 haunted at 10 min secures ~7.9/s.
- [WIKI] Haunted souls can be spent in the shop (after secured souls); they count towards level-up. If you die and lose them you stay at the last unlocked level but lose progress.
- [WIKI] The HUD number shows haunted portion with a red icon; the hero has a silver-green ball on the hip.
- [INFERENCE] Coach cue: after a big jungle haul, spend or avoid dying near an enemy until converted; best "spend" moment is when haunted > threshold and safe to shop.

## 4. When to buy
- [WIKI] Strategy: tempo (economy efficiency) matters; reach power spikes early without compromising survival; counterbuy with items (actives are often a good choice).
- [WIKI] Shops: one near each Guardian (closes once that Guardian dies), spawn room, and two neutral "secret" shops. Can shop while dead (Death page).
- [WIKI] Dying gives the enemy a bounty and costs farm time (Death page and Souls).
- [COMMUNITY] Buy to solve the next few minutes' problem: sustain if poked out, range/movement if you cannot contest, defense if one pattern kills you; reassess after each reset (deadlockatlas).
- [COMMUNITY] Spend steadily and stay alive; hoarding for one expensive item while dying is a trap (metabot).
- [COMMUNITY] Crash wave, shop, return (games.gg). Do not shop on a neutral wave (you pay a wave tax).
- [COMMUNITY] Shop after major value spikes so a fragile lead does not enter a fight unspent (games.gg).
- UNVERIFIED: ideal soul amounts for each purchase, exact first-buy timing.

## 6. Slots
- [WIKI] Extra Slots (flex slots): three, unlocked per enemy Walker destroyed (1: bottom left, 2: top right, 3: bottom right). Base slot count was increased from 8 to 9 and flex reduced from 4 to 3 on 2025-11-21.
- [WIKI] Total item slots therefore up to 12 (9 base + 3 flex). The base count of 9 is stated in the Extra Slots update history; confirm in game. 
- [COMMUNITY] Walker = "one of the highest-value post-lane objectives" because of the slot (games.gg).
- [INFERENCE] Coach cue: if flex slots are locked and unspent souls are high, remind of walker objective; if a slot just unlocked and souls are available, remind to buy.

## 7. Comeback and catch-up mechanics ([WIKI] Souls)
- Trailing team: up to +26% more souls from troopers, haunts, sacrifices, objectives (peaks at 20% net worth deficit; first 3k deficit ignored).
- Killing a hero stronger than killer team average gives up to +126.8%.
- After 10 min, killing a player >15% above the killer team's average net worth adds +6s to +22s respawn.
- Two lowest net-worth players per team gain passive souls after 8 min (2.5% and 1.5% of team income); 70% of comeback souls earned by those above average net worth are redistributed to them.
- Soul Urn gives more to the trailing team. Rift gives trailing team faster capture and extra Rift Troopers.
- Souls changelog 2026-09-16: reduced comeback bounties, Rift comeback resist now 10% + 1%/min (cap 40% at 30 min); objectives bounty share to nearby heroes 25% (rest split among six players).
- [INFERENCE] Implication: do not coach "throw souls at the lowest player" - the game already does; instead coach sustained farming.

## 8. Farming sources (Strategy)
- [WIKI] Box running: crates and golden statues; push wave first so you can return without missing trooper souls.
- [WIKI] Jungling: clear camps between trooper waves; leave one neutral alive in enemy territory to stall respawn; clear whole camp in your territory (respawn starts when all dead; Sinner's Sacrifices count).
- [WIKI] Items that help clear camps include Cultist Sacrifice and Ricochet (names only).
- [WIKI] Roaming assassin heroes can steal unsecured souls from junglers.
- [WIKI] Funneling: deliberately concentrating souls on one carry (one player collects a lane); simplest method is one person collecting lane souls.
- [WIKI] Permanent buffs: Golden Statues (50% chance per statue, 1 buff), Sinner's Sacrifice (4 on heavy melee jackpot, 1 on light), Mid-Boss (2 each), Soul Urn (4), Golden Goose Egg (1/60s). Tier 2 at 10 min, Tier 3 at 30 min.
- [WIKI] Permanent buff stats at Level 1: Fire Rate +1.5%, Max Ammo +3%, CDR +0.5%, Weapon Dmg +3%, Max Health +15, Spirit Power +2, Bullet/Spirit Resist +0.5%, Move Speed +0.1 m/s, Ability Range +0.25%.
- [COMMUNITY] Souls per minute (SPM) is the main metric for a lane (games.gg).

## 1. What laning is
- [WIKI] Laning = opening phase, pairs per lane fight to gain an advantage. Generally considered over when half or more of the towers are destroyed (Strategy page).
- [WIKI] Not every lane is winnable; mitigating a loss is valuable.
- [WIKI] Factors: matchups, resources (soul breakpoints, abilities), objectives that spawn during lane, wave priority, ganks.
- [WIKI] Lane swapping with teammates is possible in the spawn room before the match. In Ranked, the pre-match phase is 30s to switch lanes and/or change hero (once per player).
- [COMMUNITY] Map is a 3-lane map (York/Yellow, Broadway/Blue, Greenwich/Green) with 2-2-2 starting split (games.gg). Wiki's Cursed Apple page confirms the three lanes.

## 2. Trooper waves and orbs (what the HUD shows)
- [WIKI] Wave spawn every 30s (0-20 min), 25s (20-35 min), 20s after 35 min.
- [WIKI] Wave composition: initially 3 ranged + 1 medic; after 5 min: 2 ranged, 1 melee, 1 medic.
- [WIKI] Trooper bounty = 100 + 2 per minute. Split 50% ground orb / 50% floating orb.
- [WIKI] Ground orb: cannot be denied, stays 18s (scales to 40s after 10 min). Floating orb: spawns dark, can be shot once lit. Ally orbs green, enemy orbs orange.
- [WIKI] Shooting the enemy floating orb = DENY: souls go to the denier and nearby allies. Own orb unshot ~1s after popping is auto-awarded to nearby allies; lost if no allies nearby.
- [WIKI] One bullet/pellet of any damage confirms or denies; the bullet is refunded to the magazine.
- [WIKI] Melee last hit prevents the floating orb and gives the ground-orb souls AND the remaining 50% instantly (100% on kill). Abilities that deal melee damage do too.
- [WIKI] Melee can secure orbs but only after a short delay when denying enemy orbs.
- [WIKI] Soul sharing: allies within 45m of the orb or 30m of the killer share. Two players: 54% each (108% total); so staying near your partner is net-positive for souls but splits per-player amount.
- [WIKI] Souls start: every player starts with 600 souls.
- [WIKI] Post-lane denies: the denier gets all souls (Strategy page).

## 3. Last hitting and denies
- [WIKI] Denying "involves shooting the Souls of one's own trooper when they are killed" - preventing enemy orbs. It is crucial in laning and still effective later.
- [COMMUNITY] Denies are a double swing (you gain, they lose). Winning the deny war matters more than winning gunfights (metabot). Priorities for solo-ish lanes: safe last hits and denies over risky harass.
- [COMMUNITY] Don't chase kills through unfavorable waves; "a lane that ends 0-0 but with you up a full item and your Guardian untouched is a won lane" (games.gg).
- [INFERENCE] HUD signal for detection: floating orbs visible near crosshair; own souls total stagnant across multiple wave deaths.

## 4. Orbs, boxes and bounty sources early
- [WIKI] Crates break from ~3:00 (most), 60% chance to drop souls; amount = 23 + 2 x minute. Heavy melee on tough crates always drops souls. These are *haunted* souls (lost on death, see economy file).
- [WIKI] Golden statues/buff containers drop permanent buffs (Tier 1 before 10 min). Strategy page names "box running" as a way to get souls and buffs; pushing the wave into the enemy guardian gives time to box run and return without missing trooper souls.
- [WIKI] Easy camps spawn at 2:00; medium at 5:00; hard and Sinner's Sacrifice at 8:00 (Cursed Apple page, flagged outdated by the wiki).
- [WIKI] Clearing a trooper wave is generally faster and gives more souls than a neutral camp, so clear camps between waves. Camp respawn timers start only when all neutrals are dead.
- [WIKI] Soul Well in the base starts releasing 10-soul orbs after ~2:30 (Souls) / 3:00 (Cursed Apple). UNVERIFIED which is current.
- [WIKI] First blood gives +125 souls.
- [WIKI] First hero bounty: 200 souls at 0 min, +50/min (cap 2200 at 40 min). Dying early is cheap in souls but costs lane time.

## 5. Trading
- [WIKI] Cover: third-person camera is locked to the right shoulder, so peeking from the right side of cover exposes less than left. Use in lane trades (Strategy page; echoed by games.gg).
- [WIKI] Heroes lean around vertical edges exposing the head hitbox.
- [WIKI] Troopers deal deceptively high damage if you overextend (Strategy page). Trooper DPS vs players 28; players take +25% from troopers (resist -25 in the combat table).
- [COMMUNITY] Before trading, name the reward (soul secure, push enemy off a structure, force a cooldown). Trade when their key ability is on cooldown (deadlockatlas).
- [COMMUNITY] Pre-fight checklist (games.gg): stamina for an escape? Partner position known? Cooldowns available? Wave safe if you die? Can you win the next 5 seconds?

## 6. Wave states and lane equilibrium
- [WIKI] Wave priority: pushing the wave creates safe space for you, dangerous space for them.
- [WIKI] Freeze: damage enemy troopers so counts equalize; wave neither advances nor retreats. Useful to stay in safe zones of your guardian and to deny the opponent farm far from their defenses.
- [WIKI] Medic pack: healing from a medic trooper pack heals your troopers and can break a freeze. Collect it only after a few of your minions died.
- [WIKI] Pushing up lanes extends your team's ziplines.
- [WIKI] Respawn timers and wave timing: leave lane right after securing a wave; return before the next arrives.
- [COMMUNITY] Three states: hold/freeze, slow push, crash (games.gg); shove/frozen/being pushed (metabot). Both agree: crash (shove) before leaving lane so you don't pay a "wave tax".
- [COMMUNITY] Slow push: thin the enemy wave, keep yours alive, let it roll into their guardian; this becomes your rotation ticket (metabot).
- [INFERENCE] Minimap trooper positions relative to the midpoint indicate wave state.

## 7. When to rotate
- [WIKI] Ganks: players might leave a lane to assist another.
- [WIKI] Bridge buffs spawn every 5 min and are a force multiplier for the next 160s (Strategy page).
- [WIKI] Urn spawns 10:00, then every 5 min. Rift spawns ~10-12 min.
- [WIKI] Zipline boost unlocks at 6:00. Killing an enemy at 6:00 means they return faster than usual.
- [COMMUNITY] Leave when: wave crashed into enemy guardian; enemy laner died/recalled; neighbor lane winnable; objective uncontested (metabot list). Do not lane-hop on instinct during early game (metabot beginner mistakes).
- [COMMUNITY] A rotation needs a job; if none, go back to lane (deadlockatlas).

## 8. Guardian pressure
- [WIKI] Guardians are the first defensive layer, stay in their lane. Destroying a Guardian: 1375 souls (Souls table; the Strategy page text still says 1250). 25% to nearby heroes, 75% to team.
- [WIKI] Guardians lose all remaining damage resistance at 12:00.
- [WIKI] Guardian trooper DPS vs them is low (14.4); Guardian parry: a successful parry blocks its melee and stops its attack for a short time.
- [WIKI] Backdoor protection does NOT apply to lane Guardians (outside Street Brawl). Troopers still matter for pressure.
- [COMMUNITY] Pressure the guardian only with a crashed wave tanking; take short windows; retreat when troopers die: "your Troopers are your tank" (games.gg).
- [WIKI] Destroying a Guardian closes that Curiosity Shop for the owner (shop closes: "up to 6 shops can close, one per Guardian defeated" - Cursed Apple).

## 9. First item purchases and timing
- [WIKI] Items are bought in Curiosity Shops (one by each Guardian for the owning team, plus spawn rooms). The shop can be browsed while dead.
- [WIKI] Strategy page: tempo = efficiency of economy and build; reach power spikes without losing survival; counter items are good; active items are often a good choice.
- [WIKI] Ultimate can be unlocked at 3.8k total souls (Boon page; Strategy page: "ultimate abilities are available to unlock when the enemy reaches 3.8k souls, which can significantly tilt fights in lane"). Levels 0, 2, 4 and 7 grant an ability unlock; the first three unlocks can only be used on non-ultimate abilities. Per-level soul thresholds exist in the Boon table but the flattened text was ambiguous - UNVERIFIED for individual levels other than 3.8k.
- [COMMUNITY] Spend small early on what solves the lane problem: sustain if health pressure stops you, range/movement if you can't contest, defense if one pattern keeps winning trades (deadlockatlas). Don't hoard for one expensive item while dying (metabot).
- [COMMUNITY] Pick up at least one early sustain item (metabot cites "Healing Rite"; that specific name is from the community guide only - UNVERIFIED on wiki).
- [COMMUNITY] Crash wave then shop, then return (games.gg "Crash-Shop-Return").
- UNVERIFIED: exact first-buy timing in minutes, and which items to buy; no wiki item data was fetched.

## 10. Solo vs duo (community)
- [COMMUNITY] 2v1/1v2 situations occur constantly. 1v2: stop pushing, hold wave near own side, give up denies, don't die. 2v1: freeze/hold, punish pickups, slow push, crash; avoid early dives (games.gg).
- [COMMUNITY] Duo role split: one handles wave, one zones/pressures (games.gg).
- [COMMUNITY] Safe triangle: cover, wave, retreat path (games.gg).

## 11. HUD-observable lane indicators (for the coach)
- Game clock (to 6:00-ish lane phase; first urn 10:00).
- Own souls and boon level; shop-badge/unspent souls.
- Own health/stamina bars.
- Minimap: trooper dot clusters, enemy positions, "missing" markers.
- Guardian health bars; Guardian count remaining on top HUD.
- Lane icon colors (yellow/blue/green).
- [INFERENCE] Unspent souls: a large unspent souls value while alive and away from fight = candidate cue.

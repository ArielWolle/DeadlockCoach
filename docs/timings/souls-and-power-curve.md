## Starting souls and sources
- Every player starts with 600 souls.
- First Blood bonus: +125 souls.
- Hero kill bounty: 200 + 50 per minute, capped at 2200 (reached at 40 min = 2400 s).
- Trooper 100 + 2/min; Small Haunt 41 + 0.44/min; Medium Haunt 68 + 0.73/min; Large Haunt 181 + 1.95/min.
- Mid-Boss 3000 + 50/min (team split); Sinner's Sacrifice 310 + 3.35/min; Crate 23 + 2/min (60% drop chance); Tough Crate 46 + 4/min (100%, heavy melee).
- Unstable Rift 1482 + 222/min (scaling starts at min 13), team split; Soul Urn 250 + 70/min; Golden Goose Egg 400 + 80/min.
- Guardian 1375, Walker 3675, Base Guardian 1000, Shrine 2000 (team).
- Soul Well (base): 10-soul orbs every 2 s starting 2:30 (Souls page) / 3:00 (Cursed Apple page) - CONFLICT, UNVERIFIED.
- Haunts grow 1.08% of base value per minute (Souls page text).
- Haunted souls (from Haunts, crates, Sinners): lost on death above threshold 50 + 5/min -> dropped as Soul Bag (3 min); converted to secured at 0.5% of haunted pool per second plus a base drain starting 1.6 souls/s.

## Comeback / catch-up
- Losing team gets up to 26% more souls from troopers, haunts, sinners and objectives (peaks at 20% max net worth difference; first 3k difference ignored).
- Killing a hero stronger than killer team's average net worth: up to 126.8% more souls.
- After 10 min, killing a player >15% above killer team's average net worth increases their respawn: +6 s at 15% / 10 min to +22 s at 30% / 25 min.
- Lowest two net worth players per team get passive souls/s after laning phase (8 min = 480 s): 2.5% and 1.5% of team souls/s gathered.

- Spawn at 5:00 (300 s) then every 300 s; heavy melee to claim; last 160 s; scale from 5 to 40 min.

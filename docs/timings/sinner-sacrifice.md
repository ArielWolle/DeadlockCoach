# Sinner's Sacrifice

## Spawn / respawn
- First spawn at 8:00 (480 s). Value scaling starts immediately from 0:00 though.
- Respawn 300 s (5 min) after being broken (if alone at its location); at a "hybrid camp" the timer starts when all Sinner's Sacrifices are broken AND all Haunts are killed.
- 15 machines at 11 locations: 6 single; 2 camps of 1 Sinner + 2 Medium Haunts; 2 camps of 2 Sinners + 1 Medium Haunt; 1 location of 3 (Bell Tower, Chinatown).
- Sunken Plaza (Plaza District) has two Sinner sites with Haunt camps (separate camps).
- Bell Tower: hitting machines/breakables rings the bell (audible to all players); Cursed Apple page: bell can ring once every 30 s.

## Mechanics
- 500 HP, melee only. Light melee 50 dmg, heavy melee 100 dmg. Retaliates 80 dmg per melee hit (minimum 320 total to the hero, maximum 640).
- All-light = 8 hits; all-heavy = 4 hits.
- Bulbs: light melee turns off 1 bulb; heavy turns off 2. At 300 damage taken (<=3 bulbs) it picks Jackpot speed: 40% fast (blinking orange/red), 60% slow (static white).
- At 400 damage taken (<=1 bulb) it enters Jackpot mode: lights turn on left to right with 8 beats; hit while all bulbs lit = Jackpot. No retaliation during Jackpot.
- Jackpot with heavy melee = 4 random permanent buffs; with light melee = 1 buff. Missing the timing gives only the remaining souls.
- Hero ability melee damage does not hit it (e.g. Viscous Puddle Punch, Calico Leaping Slash). Baba Granny Long Legs heavy melee AoE does. Rem Lil Helpers always get full jackpots.

## Souls
- Value = 310 + 3.35 per minute (from 0:00). Examples (computed): at 8 min ~337; at 20 min ~377; at 30 min ~410.5.
- Split: light melee = total/20 (rounded); heavy melee = total/10; final hit = total/2 plus one heavy payout. Souls are haunted souls (lost on death). Multiple hitters get evenly distributed payout.
- 2025-12-16: bounty reduced 10%.

## Permanent buffs (tiers by game time)
- LVL1 (0 min), LVL2 (10 min), LVL3 (30 min). Examples: Weapon Damage +3% / +4% / +6%; Max Health +15 / +20 / +30; Spirit Power +2 / +3 / +4; Fire Rate +1.5% / +2% / +2.5%.
- Drop rates: Health 20%, Move Speed 5%, Ability Range 5%, each other 10% (pseudo-random).
